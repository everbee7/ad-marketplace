# Flashd: Questions to Confirm Before We Build

Thank you for sharing the prototype and the job description. Before development starts, we'd like to confirm how the product should work, so that what we build matches what you have in mind.

**How to answer**
- Each question has **our suggestion** in it. If you agree, just reply **"OK"**. Write more only where you'd like something different.
- If you're not sure, say **"not sure"** and we'll go with our suggestion. Everything can be adjusted later, though some changes cost more after the build has started.
- Questions marked ⭐ are the most important. We need these answered before we can start.
- Examples and sketches are welcome. Even "like how TikTok does X" helps a lot.

---

## A. The big picture

**A1 ⭐ In one or two sentences, what should a business get out of Flashd, and what should a creator get out of it?**
*Why we ask:* it helps us make the right small decisions everywhere (wording, what to show first, what to leave out).

**A2 ⭐ What has to be true on launch day for you to call version 1 a success?**
For example: "10 real businesses have uploaded ads and 20 creators have made projects", or "I can demo it to investors".
*Our suggestion:* a business and a creator can each complete their full journey on their own, with no help.

**A3. Who will be the very first users?** Do you already have businesses or creators lined up, roughly how many, and in which industries or niches?
*Why we ask:* it tells us how much sample content to prepare and which categories matter most.

**A4 ⭐ Is there a fixed launch date or event we must hit?** (a pitch, a pilot with a brand, a conference…)

**A5. Roughly how much activity do you expect in the first 3 months?**
- Businesses: ___ · Ads uploaded per month: ___
- Creators: ___ · Creator videos uploaded per month: ___
- Typical creator video length: under 1 minute / 1–5 minutes / longer
*Why we ask:* video hosting is paid by usage, so these numbers let us give you a realistic monthly running-cost estimate.

## B. The burst ads

**B1 ⭐ The brief says ads are ".05–2 seconds". We read that as half a second (0.5 s) up to 2 seconds. Is that right?**
A 0.05-second ad would be about one frame, too fast for a viewer to notice.
*Our suggestion:* 0.5 to 2 seconds. Anything outside that range is refused at upload with a clear message.

**B2. Can a burst ad have sound?** Or should it always play silently over the creator's audio?
*Our suggestion:* ads keep their own sound, and the creator's video pauses while the ad plays.

**B3. What does a business upload?**
- (a) A finished video clip they made themselves *(our suggestion)*
- (b) Just a logo or image, which we turn into a short animated clip
- (c) Both

**B4. Ad shape: vertical (phone, like TikTok), horizontal (YouTube), square, or any?**
If the ad's shape doesn't match the creator's video, we'll show it with black bars rather than cropping it.
*Our suggestion:* allow any shape, show the shape on each ad, and let creators filter by it.

**B5. What information should a business give with each ad?**
*Our suggestion:* title, description, category, a few keywords (tags), and an optional cover image.
Would you also like a website link, a promo code, or "what kind of creators we're looking for"?

**B6. Can a business change an ad after it's live?**
*Our suggestion:* text changes apply straight away. Uploading a new video sends it back for review, and the old version stays visible until the new one is approved.

**B7. Is there a limit to how many ads one business can upload?**
*Our suggestion:* no limit in version 1.

## C. Putting ads into a creator's video (the core of the product)

**C1 ⭐ When the ad appears, what happens to the creator's video?**
- (a) **Cut-in:** the creator's video pauses, the ad plays, then the video continues *(our suggestion, and what we believe "inserted" means in the brief)*
- (b) **On top:** the ad appears in a corner or over the video while it keeps playing
- (c) Both, with the creator choosing each time
A sketch or screenshot from the prototype would help a lot here.

**C2 ⭐ How many burst ads can go into one video?**
- Only one / a few (up to ___) / as many as the creator wants
*Our suggestion:* up to 10 per video, at least 1 second apart.

**C3 ⭐ Who decides *where* in the video the ad goes?**
- (a) The creator picks the exact moment on a timeline *(our suggestion)*
- (b) The system places them automatically (e.g. evenly spread)
- (c) The business chooses ("only at the start")

**C4. Can ads go right at the very start and the very end of a video,** or only in the middle? *Our suggestion:* anywhere.

**C5. Can a creator mix ads from different businesses in one video?** Can they use the same ad more than once in the same video?
*Our suggestion:* yes to both. Competing brands could appear in the same video (see E4).

**C6. Can a creator edit the ad itself?** For example: shorten it, mute it, resize it, or add their own text on it.
*Our suggestion:* no. The ad plays exactly as the business made it. This protects the brand.

**C7. Does the creator need to do anything besides placing ads?** For example: trimming their own video, adding captions or music.
*Our suggestion:* no. Flashd is for placing ads, not a full video editor. Creators edit their videos elsewhere first.

**C8. Is there anything in the prototype for this part (the editor) that we must copy exactly?** Please point to the screen(s).

## D. What the creator walks away with

**D1 ⭐ After placing the ads, what does the creator need to *do* with the result?**
- (a) Just **watch a preview** inside Flashd and save the project *(our suggestion for version 1)*
- (b) **Download one finished video file** with the ads built in, to post on TikTok/YouTube/Instagram
- (c) Post directly to social media from Flashd
Option (b) is a big piece of work on its own: roughly +1–2 weeks and extra monthly running costs. If creators need to post the result to earn anything, though, it is probably essential. Please tell us honestly which one you need for launch.

**D2. If a downloadable file is needed (now or later), should it include a Flashd watermark or logo?**

**D3. Does the business need to see the finished creator videos that use its ad?**
*Our suggestion:* not in version 1. Later, a "where my ad is used" page.

## E. Business control and approval

**E1 ⭐ Can any creator use any live ad freely, or must the business approve each creator or each video first?**
*Our suggestion:* free to use in version 1. An approval step adds a whole back-and-forth flow (request, accept, decline, notifications).

**E2. Should a business be able to limit who uses its ads?**
For example: only creators in certain niches, only family-friendly content, only certain countries, or a maximum number of uses.
*Our suggestion:* not in version 1.

**E3. Should a business be able to see which creators saved or used its ads?** Or only the totals?
*Our suggestion:* totals only (e.g. "saved 12 times, used in 5 projects").

**E4. Are there competing-brand rules?** For example: no two coffee brands in the same video.
*Our suggestion:* no rules in version 1.

## F. Money

**F1 ⭐ How will money flow eventually?** Who pays whom (business → creator, business → Flashd, subscriptions, per use, per view…)?
Even though payments are **not** in version 1, knowing the future model lets us build in a way that won't need rework.

**F2. Is version 1 completely free for everyone?** *Our suggestion:* yes. There is no payment, pricing or payout in version 1.

**F3. Will you need to show creators any numbers?** For example: "this ad pays $X per use", or a creator's reach or follower count.
*Our suggestion:* not in version 1.

## G. Accounts and sign-up

**G1. Can anyone sign up, or is it invite-only / approved by you?**
*Our suggestion:* open sign-up for both sides. Businesses' ads are checked before going live (see I1).

**G2. Should we verify that businesses are real?** (e.g. company website, manual check by you)
*Our suggestion:* no separate check. Reviewing each ad is enough for version 1.

**G3. Can one person be both a business and a creator?**
*Our suggestion:* no. They'd use two accounts with two emails.

**G4. Do businesses need team accounts** (several staff logging in to the same company)?
*Our suggestion:* no. One login per company in version 1.

**G5. Sign-in method:** email + password only, or also "Sign in with Google / Apple"?
*Our suggestion:* email + password for version 1. Google sign-in is a small add-on later.

**G6. What should a creator's profile include?** *Our suggestion:* name, photo, niche, short bio, and social links.
Do you want follower counts? (We'd have to trust what creators type, because connecting to TikTok/Instagram to verify them is a bigger job.)

## H. Browsing the marketplace

**H1 ⭐ Can people browse ads without an account** (e.g. as a showcase on the website), or only after signing up?
*Our suggestion:* only after signing up. A public showcase helps marketing, but it needs extra design and content checks.

**H2. Can businesses browse other businesses' ads?**
*Our suggestion:* yes, view-only. It helps them see what works.

**H3. How should creators find ads?**
*Our suggestion:* search by keyword, filter by category, length and shape, and sort by newest or most popular.
Anything else, such as location, brand size, or "trending"?

**H4. Please confirm the category list** (add or remove freely):
Food & Drink · Fashion & Beauty · Tech & Apps · Fitness & Health · Travel & Hospitality · Home & Living · Finance · Education · Entertainment · Gaming · Local Business · Other

**H5. Do you want "featured" ads that you choose to show at the top?** *Our suggestion:* not in version 1.

## I. Review and safety

**I1 ⭐ Who checks new ads before they go live, and how quickly?**
*Our suggestion:* every ad is checked by you (or your team) in an admin area before it appears. Aim for a decision within 24 hours.

**I2. What isn't allowed?** For example: alcohol, gambling, adult content, politics, medical claims, weapons.
Do you have a content policy, or should we draft a simple one for you to approve?

**I3. Should creators' own videos be checked too?**
*Our suggestion:* no. Creator videos are private to the creator. You can still hide anything that's reported or that you find.

**I4. Should users be able to report an ad?** *Our suggestion:* not in version 1. You can remove ads from the admin area.

**I5. How many people will run the admin side?** Do they need different permission levels?
*Our suggestion:* one admin level, for 1–3 people.

## J. Look, feel and devices

**J1 ⭐ Is the Bubble prototype the final layout, or a rough idea?** Are any screens in it outdated or missing?
Please list any screen you consider a "must-have" exactly as shown.

**J2. Do you have a logo, brand colours, fonts or a style you like?** (Links to apps or sites you admire are very useful.)
*This is our next step after these questions.*

**J3. Is "Flashd" the final name?** Do you own the domain we'll launch on?

**J4. Will creators mostly use a phone or a computer?**
*Our suggestion:* everything works on phones, but placing ads on the timeline is designed mainly for computer and tablet, with a simpler phone version.

**J5. English only?** *Our suggestion:* yes for version 1.

## K. Emails and notifications

**K1. Which emails are must-haves for launch?**
*Our suggestion:* only "confirm your email" and "reset your password".
Possible extras: "your ad was approved / rejected", "a creator used your ad", a weekly summary.

## L. Launch and running the product

**L1 ⭐ Accounts and costs.** The product runs on a few services: website hosting (Vercel), database (MongoDB), video hosting (Mux), and email (Resend), plus small helpers.
*Our suggestion:* they're created **in your name, with your card**, so you fully own everything. We'll guide you through setup, and most have free starting tiers.
Is that OK?

**L2. Do you have Terms of Service and a Privacy Policy,** or do you need them drafted for a lawyer to review? (They're needed before real users sign up.)

**L3. Should we load sample ads and videos for demos?** Can you provide real brand clips to use?

**L4. Who answers user questions after launch?** Is a simple "contact us" email enough?

**L5. How should we review progress with you?**
*Our suggestion:* three live demos: business side (end of week 1), creator side (end of week 2), and launch. We'd ask for feedback within 1 working day of each.

**M1. What are the top 3 things you'd want right after launch?** (So we can avoid building ourselves into a corner.)

**M2. Is there anything you assume is included that isn't listed anywhere above?**
